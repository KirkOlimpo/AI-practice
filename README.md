Gemini Voice Assistant
A simple Python voice assistant powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, capable of understanding voice commands and responding with spoken replies using speech_recognition, pyttsx3, and Gemini's generative AI.

Features
Passive listening for the wake word "gemini"

Converts your speech to text using Google's speech recognition

Sends the transcribed text to Gemini 2.0 for a smart AI response

Responds back to you via text-to-speech

Supports continuous conversation until you say "exit", "quit", or "stop"

Say "gemini" to activate the assistant and ask anything you want.

Stop the Assistant
Say "exit", "quit", or "stop" to end the session.
